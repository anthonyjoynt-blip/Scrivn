# Privacy policy: what we add to the generated one

The privacy policy is generated by TermsFeed and hosted there (the link is in `lib/legal.ts`). A
generator only knows generic apps, so it cannot describe what Scrivn actually handles: homeowners'
details and photos entered by restoration companies, the AI step, Scrivn Scan's camera, dictation.
Those sections are written here and pasted into the generated policy with TermsFeed's manual editor.

They are self-contained on purpose. Regenerating the policy (for example adding US laws later) means
ticking the new options in the generator and pasting these sections back in, not rewriting anything.

**Keep this file true.** When Scrivn gains a provider, a permission, a new kind of data, or a change
in where data lives, update the sections below in the same change, then paste the new text into
TermsFeed. The policy is only as accurate as this file.

Written 2026-10-01 against the code as it stood then.

## Sections to paste

---

**Information about your customers**

Scrivn is used by restoration companies to document property-damage claims. When a company's staff create a claim, they may enter information about the property owner or occupant and the loss, including: name, property address, phone number, insurer and claim number; dates and cause of loss; a dictated walkthrough; sketches, measurements and moisture readings; and photographs, including 360° images, of the property. Scrivn processes this information on behalf of the company that entered it, only to provide the Service to that company: producing scope documents and inspection reports, storing the claim, and sending documents where the company directs. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. A customer with questions about their claim should contact the restoration company handling it, and we will help that company respond.

**AI processing**

To turn a walkthrough into a scope document and inspection report, Scrivn sends the claim's text (the dictation, claim details and answers to follow-up questions) to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the Claude AI model. Anthropic processes it only to return the result and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. Photos are not sent for AI processing.

**Dictation**

When you use the Dictate button, your speech is turned into text by your phone or browser's built-in speech recognition (on Android, Google's). Scrivn receives only the text. It never receives or stores audio.

**Scrivn Scan**

Scrivn Scan uses your phone's camera to measure rooms and to take photos and 360° images. It asks for camera permission only, and does not access your location, microphone, contacts or files. Measurements and photos are sent to Scrivn when you send a scan to a claim. Scan is linked to your account by scanning a pairing code, and you can revoke a paired phone from your account at any time. You can use Scan without pairing it to a Scrivn account.

When you choose Send to Scrivn team, Scan sends us that scan's measurements, the app's recent log (what the app did and how its tracking went, for that scan and any others made since the app was opened, including their measurements, with your phone's model and its Android and app versions), a random ID made for this copy of the app, and whatever you write in the note and contact boxes. No photos are sent this way. We use these only to find and fix problems in Scan, and delete them within 12 months.

Scrivn Scan runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

**Service providers**

Your account, and your claims, photos and scans, are stored in Canada. To run Scrivn we also use the providers below, several of them in the United States, so information they handle (for example a claim's text during AI processing, or an email being sent) may be processed there:
- Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in (hosted in Canada)
- Vercel: website hosting
- Anthropic: AI processing
- Resend: email (sign-in confirmations, contact-form messages, and documents you choose to send)
- Stripe: payments
- Google: ARCore on Scrivn Scan, and speech recognition when you dictate on Android

**Keeping and deleting claim data**

Claims, photos and scans are kept until the company deletes them, or for 24 months after the company's account closes. Claims can be deleted in the app. To close your account or delete your data, email privacy@scrivn.ca.

## Why some of it is there

- **The ARCore sentence is required.** Google's ARCore terms require an app that uses it to say so in
  its privacy policy, and the Play Store listing for Scrivn Scan depends on it.
- **No analytics, SMS or marketing.** The generated policy once claimed all three. Overstating is not
  harmless: Scrivn Scan's Play Store Data safety form has to match the policy.
- **Where each fact comes from:** Anthropic: `lib/anthropic.ts` (claim details and transcript go to
  `/api/extract` and `/api/generate`, no images). Resend: `lib/email/send.ts`, used by sign-in
  confirmation, the contact form and Send documents. Dictation: `components/DictateButton.tsx` (browser
  speech recognition, no audio leaves the device to Scrivn). Scan permissions: the
  ARCapturePrototype manifest (CAMERA and INTERNET only). Phone revoke: `components/PairedPhones.tsx`.
  Send to Scrivn team: `app/api/device/reports/route.ts` and `lib/scanReports.ts` (capture, gzipped log,
  install id, note, contact; the sender's address only as a salted hash for the hourly limit), table
  `scan_reports` (0008, readable by the service role only). The 12-month deletion is a promise with no
  job behind it yet - delete old rows by hand until there is one.
