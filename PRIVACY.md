# Privacy policy: what we add to the generated one

The privacy policy is generated by TermsFeed and hosted there (the link is in `lib/legal.ts`). A
generator only knows generic apps, so it cannot describe what Scrivn actually handles: homeowners'
details and photos entered by restoration companies, the AI step, Scrivn Scan's camera, dictation.
Those sections are written here and pasted into the generated policy with TermsFeed's manual editor.

They are self-contained on purpose. Regenerating the policy (for example adding US laws later) means
ticking the new options in the generator and pasting these sections back in, not rewriting anything.

**Keep this file true.** When Scrivn gains a provider, a permission, a new kind of data, or a change
in where data lives, update the sections below in the same change, then paste the new text into
TermsFeed. The policy is only as accurate as this file.

**Information about your customers**

Scrivn is used by restoration companies to document property-damage claims. When a company's staff create a claim, they may enter information about the property owner or occupant and the loss, including: name, property address, phone number, insurer and claim number; dates and cause of loss; a dictated walkthrough; sketches, measurements and moisture readings; and photographs of the property, including 360° images and site photos with the caption, the time and the room recorded with each. Scrivn processes this information on behalf of the company that entered it, only to provide the Service to that company: producing scope documents and inspection reports, storing the claim, and sending documents where the company directs. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. A customer with questions about their claim should contact the restoration company handling it, and we will help that company respond.

**AI processing**

To turn a walkthrough into a scope document and inspection report, Scrivn sends the claim's text (the dictation, the captions of its site photos with each photo's room and time, claim details and answers to follow-up questions) to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the Claude AI model. The site photos themselves are not sent. When a user chooses AI trace on a side of the house in the exterior sketch, or Finish exterior in Scrivn Scan, Scrivn also sends Anthropic that side's photo, squared up to the wall (a photo from Scrivn Scan, or one of the claim's site photos when the outside of the house was measured from photos), so that Claude can trace the wall's cladding, windows and doors and read its roof (pitch, overhangs, gable or hip) for the user to confirm; Scrivn does not store the squared photo sent from the phone. When a user chooses Photo beside a reading in the drying log, Scrivn sends Anthropic that photo of the meter's screen, reduced in size, so that Claude can read the number for the user to check; Scrivn does not store that photo. When a user uploads a roof measurement report (a PDF, which may include the property's address and aerial images of it) on the exterior sketch, Scrivn sends Anthropic that PDF so that Claude can read the roof's measurements for the user to check and accept; Scrivn keeps the numbers read, with the report's maker, date, address and file name, but does not store the PDF. Anthropic processes this information only to return the result and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. No other photos or files are sent for AI processing.

**Dictation**

When you use the Dictate button, or speak a caption after taking a photo in Scrivn, your speech is turned into text by your phone or browser's built-in speech recognition (on Android, Google's). Scrivn receives only the text. It never receives or stores audio.

**Photos and location**

Scrivn keeps no location (GPS) information from photos. Each photo taken or added in Scrivn is redrawn at a smaller size on your device before it is stored, which leaves out the location and the other details a camera writes into a photo file. When you add a photo from your phone's gallery, Scrivn reads only the date and time it was taken, to file it in order, and, for photos used to measure the outside of a house, the camera lens's focal length, to measure from them.

**Scrivn Scan**

Scrivn Scan uses your phone's camera to measure rooms and to take photos and 360° images. It asks for camera permission only, and does not access your location, microphone, contacts or files. Measurements and photos are sent to Scrivn when you send a scan to a claim. You can use Scan without an account.

You can sign in to Scan with your Scrivn email and password, create a Scrivn account in Scan (with your name, email address and a password), or link Scan to your account by scanning a pairing code. Your password goes to Scrivn only to sign you in and is not kept on the phone; the phone keeps a token instead, which you can end with Sign out in Scan or by revoking the phone from your account. While Scan is signed in, Scrivn records an ID for each scan the first time it is shared as a plan or sent, with the time and which phone, to count your free scans each month.

When you choose Send to Scrivn team, Scan sends us that scan's measurements, the app's recent log (what the app did and how its tracking went, for that scan and any others made since the app was opened, including their measurements, with your phone's model and its Android and app versions), a random ID made for this copy of the app, and whatever you write in the note and contact boxes. No photos are sent this way. We use these only to find and fix problems in Scan, and delete them within 12 months.

Scrivn Scan runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

**Working without a connection**

So that Scrivn works with no signal, it keeps a copy of the claims you have opened recently, and of the latest ones on your claims list, in your browser's storage on your device, along with changes that have not reached our servers yet, photos that have not uploaded yet, and AI steps waiting to run. Signing out removes those copies; changes and photos that have not reached Scrivn yet stay on the device, out of sight of anyone else who signs in there, until you sign in again, so that they are not lost. Scrivn Scan likewise keeps a scan it could not send, with its photos, on the phone until it has been sent or you delete it.

**Service providers**

Your account, and your claims, photos and scans, are stored in Canada. To run Scrivn we also use the providers below, several of them in the United States, so information they handle (for example a claim's text during AI processing, or an email being sent) may be processed there:
- Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in (hosted in Canada)
- Vercel: website hosting
- Anthropic: AI processing
- Resend: email (sign-in confirmations and other account emails, contact-form messages, and documents you choose to send)
- Stripe: payments
- Google: ARCore on Scrivn Scan, and speech recognition when you dictate on Android

**Keeping and deleting claim data**

Claims, photos and scans are kept until the company deletes them, or for 24 months after the company's account closes. Claims and photos can be deleted in the app; deleting a claim deletes its photos and scans with it.

**Deleting your account**

You can delete your account yourself, in Scrivn Scan (Account, then Delete account) or at scrivn.ca/account/delete, or by emailing privacy@scrivn.ca from the address on the account. You'll be asked for your password. Deleting your account deletes at once your sign-in, your name and contact details, the phones linked to your account, and problem reports you sent from Scan while signed in. If nobody else is in your company's account, it also deletes the company's claims, photos, scans, letterhead and logo, and cancels its subscription straight away (the time already paid for is not refunded). If other people are in your company's account, you leave it, and the claims stay with the company. We keep payment records, which Stripe holds as tax law requires, and a one-way scrambled copy of your email address (see below).

**Security and preventing abuse**

To protect accounts and limit abuse, Scrivn counts attempts to create an account and to sign in to Scan, keyed by a one-way scrambled copy (a hash) of the internet address and of the email address used, which cannot be turned back into either; these counts are deleted within two days. To stop the free trial being claimed more than once, Scrivn keeps a one-way scrambled copy of the email address of each account created, including after the account is deleted. Scrivn also keeps a daily total of what its AI processing costs, which holds no personal information.

## Why some of it is there

- **The ARCore sentence is required.** Google's ARCore terms require an app that uses it to say so in
  its privacy policy, and the Play Store listing for Scrivn Scan depends on it.
- **No analytics, SMS or marketing.** The generated policy once claimed all three. Overstating is not
  harmless: Scrivn Scan's Play Store Data safety form has to match the policy.
- **Where each fact comes from:** Anthropic: `lib/anthropic.ts` (claim details and transcript go to
  `/api/extract` and `/api/generate`, no images; since 2026-10-12 a side's squared-up exterior photo
  goes to `/api/exterior/trace` when the user presses AI trace, `lib/exteriorTrace.ts`, and since 2026-10-15 to
  `/api/device/exterior/trace` from the phone when the user presses Finish exterior (squared on the phone by
  `lib/scanEngine.ts`, kept nowhere; `lib/traceCall.ts`); since 2026-10-14
  a photo of a meter's screen, shrunk to 1280 px in the browser, goes to `/api/drying/meter-photo` when
  the user presses Photo in the drying log, `lib/meterPhoto.ts`, and is kept nowhere; since 2026-10-14
  the site photos' captions, as text with each one's room and time, go to `/api/extract` and
  `/api/generate` beside the transcript, `lib/photoNotesPrompt.ts` — never the pictures; since 2026-10-15
  a roof report's PDF, as uploaded and up to 4.4 MB, goes to `/api/exterior/roof-report` when the user
  presses Read a roof report on the exterior's plan, `lib/roofReport.ts`, and is kept nowhere; what was
  read rides in the claim as `ExteriorRoof.report`). Resend: `lib/email/send.ts`, used by sign-in
  confirmation, the contact form and Send documents. Dictation: `components/DictateButton.tsx` (browser
  speech recognition, no audio leaves the device to Scrivn). Scan permissions: the
  ARCapturePrototype manifest (CAMERA and INTERNET only). Phone revoke: `components/PairedPhones.tsx`.
  Send to Scrivn team: `app/api/device/reports/route.ts` and `lib/scanReports.ts` (capture, gzipped log,
  install id, note, contact; the sender's address only as a salted hash for the hourly limit), table
  `scan_reports` (0008, readable by the service role only). The 12-month deletion is a promise with no
  job behind it yet - delete old rows by hand until there is one.
- **Scan accounts, the free scans, deleting and the guards** (2026-10-14, migration 0014): signing in
  from the phone is `app/api/device/signin` (the password is checked with Supabase from the server,
  `lib/passwordSignIn.ts`, and the phone keeps the device token, `PairingStore.kt`); creating an
  account, in Scan or on the website, is `lib/signup.ts` (name, email, password; Scrivn sends the
  confirmation email through Resend). The free-scan count is `scan_uses` (capture id, time, user,
  phone, sent or saved; `lib/scanAllowance.ts`). Deleting is `lib/accountDeletion.ts` with
  `account_close` (0014), reached from `AccountScreen.kt` and `app/(app)/account/delete`. The guards
  are `request_limits` (salted hashes of address and email, swept after two days,
  `lib/requestGuard.ts`) and `trial_emails` (salted hash of the normalised email, kept after deletion);
  `ai_spend_days` holds token counts and dollars per day only. The Play Data safety form must say:
  email address, name and purchase history collected (account management, and app functionality for
  purchases), user IDs and app interactions (the scan count) collected for app functionality, data
  deletion available in the app and at scrivn.ca/account/delete — see SCAN_ACCOUNTS.md.
- **Site photos** (2026-10-14): the camera and the Photos panel are `components/photos/*`; a photo is
  redrawn to about 2,000 px on a canvas before it is kept (`lib/photoImage.ts`), which is why no GPS
  or other EXIF data is stored. Pictures are in the private `claim-photos` bucket (0015), written only
  by the server (`lib/claimPhotos.ts`), readable by the organization's members; records (room, time,
  caption) ride in the claim (`lib/photos.ts`). Captions use the same browser speech recognition as
  Dictate (`lib/useDictation.ts`). A gallery photo's taken time is read from its EXIF
  `DateTimeOriginal`/`OffsetTimeOriginal` and nothing else (`lib/exifTime.ts`). Since 2026-10-16 a photo
  added to measure the outside of a house (From photos, `components/sketch/PhotoRoute.tsx`) also has its
  EXIF `FocalLengthIn35mmFilm` read, nothing more (`lib/photoRouteExif.ts`); those photos are claim photos
  (`SidePhoto.claimPhotoId`), and AI trace can send one, squared, to `/api/exterior/trace` (refused unless
  `SCRIVN_EXTERIOR_READS=on`, `lib/traceCall.ts`). Deleting a claim removes its `claim-photos` folder and its scans'
  `scan-photos` folders (`removeClaimFolders`; the scan photos were left behind before this). Photos
  waiting to upload are the `photoFiles` store in IndexedDB (`lib/devicePhotos.ts`), not swept on
  sign-out.
- **Working without a connection** (2026-10-13, offline mode): the device copies are IndexedDB
  (`lib/deviceClaims.ts`, `lib/aiJobs.ts`); signing out sweeps the copies the server already has
  (`forgetCopiedClaims` in `public/sw.js`) and keeps unsynced work, scoped to its owner by
  `deviceUser`. Scan's waiting scans are its send queue (files in the app's private storage).
